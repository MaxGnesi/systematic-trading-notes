# Lecture 26: sources checked at source (2026-11-13)

- Moskowitz, T. J., Ooi, Y. H. and Pedersen, L. H. (2012). Time series momentum. *Journal of Financial Economics* 104(2), 228-250. doi:10.1016/j.jfineco.2011.11.003. 58 liquid futures; "persistence in returns for one to 12 months that partially reverses over longer horizons"; one rule for every instrument, the 12-month lookback "following the convention used in the cross-sectional momentum literature" (a full grid of 1-48 months is reported); sample 1985-2009; positions at 40% ex ante volatility. The paper does not say "rather than tuning per market". Confirmed: Crossref, OpenAlex, author PDF.
- Hurst, B., Ooi, Y. H. and Pedersen, L. H. (2017). A century of evidence on trend-following investing. *Journal of Portfolio Management* 44(1), 15-29. doi:10.3905/jpm.2017.44.1.015. 67 markets (29 commodities, 11 equity indices, 15 bond markets, 12 currency pairs), 1880-2016; "an equal-weighted combination of 1-month, 3-month, and 12-month time-series momentum strategies"; positions sized to the same volatility (equal risk across markets, not equal weight); "simple, without many of the often arbitrary choices of more complex models". Confirmed: Crossref, OpenAlex, published PDF.
- Meinshausen, N. and Bühlmann, P. (2010). Stability selection. *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series B* 72(4), 417-473. doi:10.1111/j.1467-9868.2010.00740.x. Random subsamples of half the data without replacement; keep variables with high selection probability; results "vary surprisingly little for sensible choices in a range of the cutoff"; error control under assumptions (exchangeability). Confirmed: OpenAlex; method from arXiv 0809.2932.
- Bailey, D. H., Borwein, J. M., López de Prado, M. and Zhu, Q. J. (2017). The probability of backtest overfitting. *Journal of Computational Finance* 20(4), 39-69. doi:10.21314/JCF.2016.322 (online 2016). Combinatorially symmetric cross-validation; PBO = probability that the configuration best in sample underperforms the median out of sample. Confirmed: publisher page; definitions from the 2015 author version.
- Data: 3-month Treasury bill rate, FRED series DTB3 (Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System), free.
